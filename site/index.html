Sales aren't profit. Delivered orders are.

Most shop dashboards count a COD order the moment it's placed. Lavkhata waits for the courier, then takes off every real cost.

1
Delivered

Sale is counted. Product cost, delivery charge, 1% COD fee and packaging are taken off.

2
Returned

No sale. You still pay delivery, return charge and packaging, so they're counted as losses.

3
Ads and overheads

Ad spend in dollars is converted at your card rate with VAT. Rent, salaries and software come off too.

Questions

Will the courier status change my orders by itself?

Only when you allow it. New installs start in "Report only" mode: you see the courier status and a note on each order, but nothing changes. Switch to "Auto-update" when you're comfortable.

How does it know if a customer is risky?

It looks up the phone number's delivery record with Steadfast and Pathao (using your own API keys) and in your own shop's history. If the number has received fewer parcels than your minimum delivery rate, the customer is asked for an advance.

Do I need a payment gateway for the advance?

No. Customers send money to your bKash, Nagad or Rocket number and enter the transaction ID. You tap "I received it" on the order. If you already use an online gateway, risky customers can pay in full there.

Does it work on Shopify?

Not yet. Lavkhata is a WordPress plugin for WooCommerce.

Does it file my VAT return?

No. It gives you a monthly P&L with every fee and a CSV your accountant can use.

Is it in Bangla?

Yes. Every screen is translated. It follows your site language, or you can pick English or বাংলা in the settings.
